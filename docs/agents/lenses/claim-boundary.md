# Claim-boundary lens

For every material conclusion, state:

- what the evidence establishes;
- what it does not establish;
- the candidate, source, oracle, or observation basis;
- missing or failed instruments;
- what later change would invalidate the conclusion;
- whether the user-facing or PR wording exceeds the proof.

Use `NOT_PROVEN` where evidence is unavailable, partial, stale, contradictory, or instrument-failed. Do not convert missing evidence into an empty green result.
