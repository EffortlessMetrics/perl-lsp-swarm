Confirmed real: with `note()` releasing the family lock before `show_message`, caller B could take `Suppress` against A's reservation and then A's failed send could `forget` it, leaving neither call's warning delivered until a later occurrence — a narrow regression against the pre-#9769 lock-across-send atomicity.

Repaired in 451a190f6: the store gained `emit_once_with(family, identity, emit)`, which decides, emits, and rolls the retention back inside one family-lock hold; `notify_ai_auth_failure` now goes through it (send result drives the rollback). Covered by `guarded_emission_holds_the_reservation_through_the_send`: a failed send leaves `entries == 0` (retryable), a successful send retains and later occurrences suppress.
