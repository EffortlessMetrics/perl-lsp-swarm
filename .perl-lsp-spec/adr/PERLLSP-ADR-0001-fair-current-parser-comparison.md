# PERLLSP-ADR-0001 — Fair Current Parser Comparison

## Status
Accepted

## Decision
perl-lsp may retain historical parser-comparison evidence, but any claim about another **current** parser requires running that current parser target through the parser-comparison harness and publishing a receipt.

## Consequences

- Historical docs remain valuable but must carry claim-boundary labeling.
- Current-upstream parser comparisons become reproducible and reviewable.
- Track A introduces process rails before behavior or policy promotion.
